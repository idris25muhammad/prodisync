Student Outcomes Assessment Plan

{% set default_metode_evaluasi = '
  1. Learning Method:
  2. Knowledge discovery: research project.
  3. Reinforcement of disciplinary knowledge: class lab / experiment, teaching in labs, self-directed learning, lecture/presentation in labs, interactive electronic class mode, distance learning mode.
  4. Product and system design and implementation: advanced design-implement project, simple design-implement project, extracurricular design project, test & operate mode, tinkering mode, linked projects.
  5. Auxiliary uses: research design support, income generating mode, outreach mode.
  6. Assessment Method:
  7. Written and oral question: use concept questions to determine students’ deeper level of understanding of disciplinary content.
  8. Performance ratings: assessed by observing students in the performance of specific tasks, for example, oral communication and teamwork. In these situations, rating scales and rubrics facilitate the collection and analysis of assessment data.
  9. Product reviews: assessment can be conducted by judging the demonstration (performance), or reviewing the physical product – be it an artifact, report, or computer drawing.
  10. Journal and portofolios: Journals and portfolios provide records of students’ individual and collaborative efforts in design-implement projects, and experimental research. These documents provide evidence of student achievement in situations where there may be no final tangible product.
  11. Self-report instruments: self-report measures, such as inventories and questionnaires. Asking students to reflect on their learning experiences helps them to see more clearly the connections among the concepts they have learned, as well as the applications of these concepts to new situations.
